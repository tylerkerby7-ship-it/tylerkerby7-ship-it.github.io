ULTRON

Ultron is a personal voice assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. It is a private, non-commercial project used by a single person and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Ultron can read their Gmail, write drafts, send email after a spoken confirmation, and read and update their Google Calendar, so they can ask things like "any new emails?" or "what's on my calendar today?".
